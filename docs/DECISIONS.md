# ARCHITECTURE & PRODUCT DECISIONS — v1.0

## DEC-001 — The game is not a conventional prediction pool
Status: APPROVED

The product must preserve a differentiated performance-aware prediction model.

Reason:
This is the core product identity.

---

## DEC-002 — xG is a protected gameplay concept
Status: APPROVED

Expected goals (xG) can participate in evaluation of prediction quality.

Reason:
The project is intended to evaluate underlying match performance, not only the final score.

---

## DEC-003 — Attacker and goalkeeper performance matter
Status: APPROVED

The design explicitly considers attacker and goalkeeper performance.

Reason:
This enables richer evaluation of what happened during the match.

---

## DEC-004 — Match phases matter
Status: APPROVED

First-half and second-half events/performance may be evaluated separately.

Reason:
The gameplay concept includes dynamic match progression.

---

## DEC-005 — RPG-like progression is part of the product direction
Status: APPROVED

The game should include progression/game-feel elements beyond a one-off prediction.

Exact mechanics remain TBD.

---

## DEC-006 — Scoring must be explainable and reproducible
Status: APPROVED

The scoring engine should produce an auditable result and be reproducible from the relevant stored data.

---

## DEC-007 — Do not simplify the product without approval
Status: APPROVED

A technically simpler implementation must not replace an approved game mechanic.

---

## DEC-008 — v1.0 deliberately avoids invented formulas
Status: APPROVED

Where the exact earlier formula is not available in this recovered memory, it is marked TBD rather than fabricated.

Reason:
False precision could permanently corrupt the product design.
